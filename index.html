Decoding where and when proteins are made

Luo Lab

@ Yale School of Medicine · Department of Cell Biology

Cells don’t just make proteins; they make them in the right place at the right time. The Luo Lab seeks to understand how cells decide where and when to make proteins, and how this control regulates fundamental biological processes. We develop cutting-edge genome-wide technologies and combine them with functional screens, biochemistry, and fluorescence imaging to reveal how spatiotemporal regulation of protein synthesis shapes cellular function in health and disease.
