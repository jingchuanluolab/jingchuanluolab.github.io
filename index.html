How cells decide where to make their proteins

The Luo lab seeks to understand how cells build and maintain compartmentalized structures, with a focus on the spatiotemporal regulation of protein synthesis in development, neurons, organelles and stress.

Four questions we ask

All research →
01 Mechanism How are mRNAs targeted to specific subcellular sites, and how is translation controlled once they arrive? 02 Function Why make proteins at specific locations rather than in the cytosol, and how does this change in stress and disease? 03 Diversity and evolution Do different compartments and different species share strategies for regulating local translation? 04 Engineering Can we harness this regulation to build synthetic organelles with new functions?
